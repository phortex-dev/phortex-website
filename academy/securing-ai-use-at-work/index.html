Securing AI Use at Work

Shadow AI, data leaks, the account and retention settings that matter, and one page of AI rules your team will actually follow.

What you’ll learn

  • Explain where your words and files go when you type them into an AI assistant, and how business accounts differ from free personal ones.
  • Find the AI tools your team is already using, including AI features built into software you already pay for.
  • Sort company information into what is safe to share, what needs care, and what never goes in.
  • Turn on the account settings that matter most: training, history and retention, shared links, and connected apps.
  • Put a one-page set of AI rules in place that your team will actually follow.

Who it’s for

People who run or work in a small or medium business where staff already use AI assistants such as ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Google Gemini or Claude. That includes the owner, office manager or IT lead who sets the rules. No technical background needed.
