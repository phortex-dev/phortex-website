This policy explains what personal information Phortex LLC collects through phortex.com, our phone line, text messages, email and booking tools, and how we use and protect it. We are a security firm; we try to collect as little as we need.

1. Who we are

Phortex LLC (“Phortex,” “we,” “us”) is a cybersecurity and AI consulting company based in Doylestown, Pennsylvania. You can reach us at contact@phortex.com or 267-641-1557.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us: your name, email address, phone number, company, and anything you write in a form, email, text, call or booking request. If you become a client, we also receive the business information needed to deliver the engagement, under a separate written agreement.

Payment information: payments are handled by a third-party payment processor. We receive confirmation of payment and limited details such as the last four digits of a card; we do not store full card numbers.

Information collected automatically: when you visit the site, our hosting provider records technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

Tools and demos: interactive tools on this site, such as the post-quantum exposure check and toolkit demo, run in your browser and do not send what you enter to us unless you submit a form.

3. How we use it

  • To answer your inquiry and schedule consultations
  • To deliver, bill for and support services you buy
  • To send appointment reminders and service updates you agreed to receive
  • To email you about the PQC Toolkit, the Learning Academy or our newsletter if you sign up (unsubscribe anytime)
  • To keep the site and our systems secure
  • To meet legal, tax and accounting obligations

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted advertising.

4. Text messages (SMS)

If you opt in, we may text you about your inquiry, appointments and services. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of any purchase.

No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are excluded from all of the sharing described in this policy and will not be shared with any third parties, except the service providers that send our messages on our behalf.

5. Phone calls

Calls to our number may be answered by an automated assistant. Calls may be recorded and transcribed for quality, scheduling and record-keeping; we tell you at the start of the call, and you can ask for a person or hang up at any time. Recordings and transcripts are stored with our communications provider and handled under this policy.

6. How we share it

We share personal information only with service providers that help us run the business, under terms that limit their use of it, and when the law requires. Our current providers are:

  • GoHighLevel: customer records, phone, text messaging and automated call answering
  • Microsoft 365: email, calendar and Microsoft Bookings scheduling
  • Cloudflare: website hosting, security and email routing
  • Formspree: website contact and waitlist forms
  • Our payment processor: payments for services and products

If Phortex is ever merged or sold, information may transfer to the new owner under the same protections.

7. Cookies and analytics

We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don’t run third-party analytics on this site. Some tools remember your choices in your own browser (for example, the steps you tick in the post-quantum action plan); that information stays on your device. If we add analytics in the future, we’ll update this policy first.

8. Retention and security

We keep inquiry records for up to 24 months after our last contact, keep client records for as long as the engagement and the law require, and delete or de-identify the rest. We protect information with access controls, multi-factor authentication, encryption in transit and at rest where our providers support it, and least-privilege access. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects you, we will notify you as the law requires.

9. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you, by emailing contact@phortex.com or calling 267-641-1557. Every marketing email has an unsubscribe link, and STOP works for texts. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under state law; we will honor them and won’t treat you differently for using them. We may need to verify your identity first.

10. Children

Our services are for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes and contact

We will post any changes here and update the date at the top. For significant changes we will also give notice on the site or by email.
