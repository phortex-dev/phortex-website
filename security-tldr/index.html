Hottest this week
#1Crypto theftTop story

Binance loses about $6.3 billion after an engineer's laptop is compromised

Attackers used access from a DevOps engineer's hacked laptop to push unauthorized withdrawals on October 29. Binance says customers will be covered; analysts tie the theft to North Korea's TraderTraitor group.

Do this: Give admin and engineering laptops your strongest protection: phishing-resistant MFA, endpoint protection, and no standing access to production or money-moving systems.

BleepingComputer · Oct 30 →
#2Firewall & VPNHot

Cisco firewall VPN flaw is being used to knock devices offline

Cisco patched an actively exploited bug in ASA and FTD firewalls that lets an outside attacker crash the device when the SSL VPN uses secondary authentication.

Do this: Update Cisco ASA or FTD firewalls to Cisco's fixed release for your version, and check every VPN tunnel group, including the default one, for secondary authentication.

The Hacker News · Oct 30 →
#3Firewall & VPNHot

Ivanti ships more fixes for exploited Connect Secure VPN flaws

On October 29 Ivanti released patches for the remaining flaws in a chain attackers used to get root on its VPN and steal Active Directory credentials.

Do this: Upgrade Ivanti Connect Secure to 22.8R2.6 or 25.1.1.3, then reset the passwords of accounts the VPN uses to reach Active Directory.

#4Browser & appsNotable

319 Chrome extensions caught rerouting web searches

Researchers found extensions posing as PDF converters, safe-search and AI tools, with 13.4 million installs, sending searches through servers that could redirect users to phishing pages.

Do this: Review the Chrome extensions on work computers, remove PDF, search or security add-ons nobody needs, then run a full antivirus scan.

#1RansomwareHot

SafePay ransomware has hit 600+ organizations

A new FBI and CISA advisory says SafePay gets in through stolen or guessed passwords, VPN access and unpatched edge devices, then encrypts everything it can reach.

Do this: Turn on MFA for every account, starting with VPN and remote access, and keep an offline backup you have actually restored from.

#2Patch nowHot

Critical flaw in Veeam Backup & Replication 13

An unauthenticated attacker can bypass authorization checks in Veeam's backup server. Backups are the first thing ransomware crews go after.

Do this: Update to 13.1.2 or 13.0.3 now. Version 12 is not affected.

#3Patch nowNotable

Microsoft ships emergency .NET security updates

Out-of-band fixes for .NET 8, 9 and 10 close a memory flaw and cover fixes the October patch release missed.

Do this: Update .NET on your servers, then rebuild any self-contained apps, since they carry their own copy.
