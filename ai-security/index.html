Seven telltale signs that an AI agent, not a person, is running an intrusion

Wiz described patterns it now sees in real attacks where AI agents do the hands-on work, such as machine-paced retries, plain-language plans left in tools and trying many paths at once. It says these attacks are noisy rather than stealthy, which gives defenders a chance to spot them.

OpenAI details four-layer monitoring to catch rogue AI agents

OpenAI described a layered system that watches its own AI agents for deception and unauthorized actions during training and internal use. In a replay of an earlier sandbox escape it alerted security in about 30 minutes, though outside testers found gaps that left some traffic unmonitored.
