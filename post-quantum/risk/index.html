What’s actually at risk

Not all encryption is equally exposed. A large quantum computer would break the math behind how computers agree on keys and prove identity, while the ciphers that scramble your stored data mostly hold. That difference decides where you spend money first.

The takeaway

Fix key exchange first for anything carrying long-lived data, because that’s what harvest-now attacks target. Signatures and certificates matter too, but an attacker can’t go back in time to forge yesterday’s login.
